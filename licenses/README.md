The Kenney colour palettes (`Textures/colormap.png` in the four Kenney folders) are made more
colourful by `tools/make_strong_colors.py` (CC0 allows changes); the originals are in `art/kenney_colors/`.

Made for this project (no third-party rights): sound effects in `assets/sfx/` (generated by
`tools/make_sfx.py`), icons in `assets/icons/`, the sprites in `assets/fx/`, the Kurvenkurs road mesh,
the toy models built in Blender from simple shapes by `tools/blender/build_duplo_*.py` and
`build_toy_vehicle.py` (Duplo girl, builder woman, plane, ambulance, train car; airliner),
and the loading screen, logo and app icon in `assets/ui/` (Blender renders of our toy models and of
3D text in the Windows font Segoe UI Black, composed with Pillow: `tools/blender/render_splash_toys.py`,
`render_logo_letters.py`, `tools/make_logo.py`, `make_splash.py`, `make_app_icon.py`). The Kenney
credit lives in this folder; the loading screen no longer shows the Starter Kit splash.

Toy photos in `photos/toys/` and the cut-outs in `assets/paper/` are private family photos of toys.
Some toys are other companies' brands; replace them before any public release (see docs/BACKLOG.md).
